Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version. Zodiey has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We don’t collect or sell your personal information. What you tell the app (your sign, birthday, music taste, saved stations and Moon log) is stored only on your device. To play radio, TV and podcasts and to search for places, the app connects directly to public services, which are listed below.

Who we are

Zodiey (“we”, “us”) makes the Zodiey app for iPhone and iPad. If you have a question about privacy, contact us at support@zodiey.com.

What stays on your device

The following is saved only in the app’s storage on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive it and it isn’t sent to any server of ours (we don’t have one).

  • Your name (optional), zodiac sign, and birth date, time and place (optional)
  • Your music styles, interests, blocked words and added sounds
  • Saved stations, saved sky events, recently played stations and what the app has learned about your taste
  • Your Moon log (how your nights felt) and notification settings

You can erase all of it at any time in Settings › Delete my data, or by deleting the app.

Services the app connects to

To work, Zodiey contacts these public services directly from your device. Like any website or app connection, they can see your device’s IP address and the request being made. We don’t send them your name, birthday or other personal details.

Location, compass and motion

Location is optional. If you choose “Use my location” or the compass in “Find in the sky”, iOS asks your permission first. Your location and the phone’s compass and tilt are used on your device to show where the Moon and planets are in your sky. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Notifications

If you turn notifications on, Zodiey schedules them on your device (for example, a reminder on a full moon). No notification server is involved and no data leaves your phone for this.

Home screen widget

The widget shows your sign, name and tonight’s station. The app shares this with the widget on your device only.

Purchases

Zodiey Plus is not on sale yet. When it is, purchases will be handled by Apple through your Apple ID. We will not see your payment details.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Zodiey contains no analytics tools, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Children

Zodiey is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Astrology

Sky positions and dates are real astronomy. Readings and meanings are astrology, written for fun. They aren’t predictions or professional advice.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Zodiey handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be explained in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@zodiey.com.
