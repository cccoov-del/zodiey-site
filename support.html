Support

Need a hand? Here are quick answers. Still stuck? Email support@zodiey.com.

A station isn’t playing, or keeps repeating

Radio stations sometimes go offline or loop a message. Open the player (tap the bar at the bottom) and press Not working?. Zodiey skips it and won’t pick it again for a few days. If a station just isn’t your style, press Not for me.

No sound

Check your volume and that another app isn’t playing. Zodiey plays even when your iPhone is on silent and keeps going when the screen locks.

Notifications

Go to Settings › Notifications › Turn on notifications in Zodiey. If you said no before, turn them on in your iPhone’s Settings › Zodiey › Notifications. You’ll get at most one a day.

My Moon and Rising signs

Add your birth date, time and place in My Sign › Edit birth details. Your Moon sign needs your birth date; your Rising sign and houses also need the time and place.

The home screen widget

Press and hold your home screen, tap Edit then Add Widget, and search for Zodiey. Open the app once so the widget knows your sign and station.

Changing my music or sign

Tap the settings icon at the top right of the app. You can change your sign, birth details, music styles, interests and blocked words any time.

Deleting my data

Everything is stored on your device. Go to Settings › Delete my data to erase it, or delete the app. See our privacy policy.

Restoring Zodiey Plus

Zodiey Plus isn’t on sale yet. When it is, use Settings › Restore purchases on a new device. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.
